Sources

  • Land cover: NASA MODIS via GIBS (500 m, yearly; model-classified, so single cells can be wrong), cross-checked against USGS / MRLC NLCD.
  • Elevation: USGS 3DEP and NASA SRTM via OpenTopoData.
  • Imagery: NASA GIBS worldwide (coarse), USGS National Map in the US; compare in Google Earth.
  • Species: GBIF records, IUCN status, iNaturalist and USFWS ECOS for checking. Places: OpenStreetMap and GeoNames.
  • Costs: Florida DOT road costs, a WSDOT crossing study, and an older ARC Solutions fence figure. Other cost lines are assumptions.
  • Impact points: a screening estimate, not a legal decision. Only wetland fill (Clean Water Act 404 / Nationwide Permit 14) and road grade (AASHTO) use official limits (★); the rest are model estimates.
